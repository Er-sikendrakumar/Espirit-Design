Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about partnering with Espirit Technologies for software development and offshore staffing.

Who owns the intellectual property (IP) and source code?

+
You retain 100% full legal ownership of all intellectual property, source code, repositories, architectural documents, and assets from Day 1. We operate under strict bilateral NDAs and execute formal IP assignment agreements before any code is written.

How does the 2-Week Risk-Free Trial Sprint work?

+
When you hire an offshore squad or commence a project with Espirit, you test our developers and velocity for 14 calendar days. If for any reason you are not satisfied with the deliverables or communication, you may walk away with zero financial obligation.

How do you handle US and international timezone differences?

+
Our engineering teams in Hyderabad work overlapping shifts guaranteeing 4 to 6 hours of real-time overlap with US Eastern, Central, and Pacific business hours. Your team has direct Slack, Jira, and Google Meet access for daily standups.

How quickly can an offshore squad or developer be onboarded?

+
For standard tech stacks (React, Node, Python, Flutter, Laravel, .NET), we can introduce pre-vetted senior profiles within 24-48 hours. Following your technical interview and green light, engineers can be deployed inside your sprints within 3-5 business days.

What flexible engagement models do you offer?

+
We provide three primary models tailored to your requirements: 1) Dedicated Engineering Squads (monthly sprint-based dedicated pods), 2) Fixed-Scope Milestone Delivery (ideal for defined MVPs with firm budgets), and 3) Time & Materials (on-demand technical advisory and maintenance).
